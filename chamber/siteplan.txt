Site Name: Maricopa Chamber of Commerce

Site purpose: provides info about local events, networking opportunities, business directories, and ads for local shopping.
Aims to promote local business growth and support local business.

Target Market: New and established residents, business owners, and entrepeneurs. 

Goal: attract new businesses and make it as easy as possible for businesses to connect via networking and  provide information needed
to grow a business. Also promote local economic growth.

Possible users: small business owners, corporate officers, new residents

Scenarios: Frank, 35, just came into town and wants to start a business in the recycling sector. He has an idea to use cooking oil to extract silver from electronics.
            Sasha, corporate sales officer for State Insurance, wants to know of networking opportunies her salesmen can attend.
            Martina, a local pastry chef owner, wants to expand her bakery to another location, and figure out what local festivities to attend to promote her bakery. 
            

SEO plan: Use relevant keywords in site descriptions, content, and blog posts.
Verify the site on Google's Business Profile.
Get inbound links or backlinks from all member business to improve rankings.
Embed Google Analytics in all site pages.
